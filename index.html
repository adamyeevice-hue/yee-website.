About

Yee. is a Hong Kong-based electronic music community and events platform built around the belief that good music builds real connections.

We document, share, and celebrate the underground — from warehouse parties to late-night listening sessions, from hardware-obsessed producers to DJs who treat every set as a conversation with the room.

Through exclusive mixes on Mixcloud, broadcasts on HKCR, in-depth writing on Substack, and events on Resident Advisor, Yee. is building a long-form record of Hong Kong's electronic music culture — the sounds, the spaces, and the people who make it.

Follow on Instagram @yee.___hk · Subscribe on Substack · Get in touch at hello@yee.hk
