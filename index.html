About
Yee.

Yee. is a music and community platform spotlighting the sounds of the underground from Hong Kong to the world.

We curate, share and celebrate the underground — from warehouse parties to listening sessions, to DJs who treat every set as a conversation with the room.

Through exclusive mixes on Mixcloud, broadcasts on HKCR, video content on YouTube, in-depth writing on Substack, and events on Resident Advisor, Yee. is building a record of Hong Kong's electronic music culture — the sounds, the spaces, and the people who make it.

Follow on Instagram @yee.___hk · Subscribe on Substack · Get in touch at yee_hk@outlook.com

Yee. 是以香港為根的音樂社群平台，記錄並慶祝地下音樂文化。從工廈派對到深夜聆聽，從硬件發燒友到以每個 set 與人對話的 DJ——我們都在乎。透過 Mixcloud、HKCR、YouTube、Substack 及 RA，Yee. 正為香港電子音樂文化建立一套長期紀錄——那些聲音、空間，以及成就一切的人。
