About

Yee. is a music and community platform spotlighting the sounds of the underground from Hong Kong to the world.

We curate high-energy events for the city, spotlight local talent, and create spaces where beats, culture, and connection thrive. Yee. is all about pushing the culture forward — together.

Through exclusive mixes on Mixcloud, broadcasts on HKCR, video content on YouTube, in-depth writing on Substack, and events on Resident Advisor, Yee. is building a long-form record of Hong Kong's electronic music culture — the sounds, the spaces, and the people who make it.
